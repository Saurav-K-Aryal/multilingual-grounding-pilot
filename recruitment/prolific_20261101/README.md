# Dated public recruitment-pool observations

On November 1, 2026, the public Prolific audience checker showed 701 first-language Swahili participants, 1,872 fluent Swahili participants and 70 first-language Twi participants active within 90 days, with no other filter. These are separate, potentially overlapping self-reported pools, not guaranteed qualified recruits. The Swahili counts changed from 700/1,870 during the same observation session, illustrating their time-varying nature.

For Swahili, each `*_filter_selected.txt` records the selected response while the corresponding `*_count*_visible.txt` and JPG record the applied-filter count. Twi's text record includes the selected response and count; its screenshot is a selection view and does not independently expose the count. Yoruba had no direct response option in the inspected language filters; this is not a zero-speaker estimate.

No account, study, participant recruitment, participant data collection or payment was created. These records support the future research recruitment plan only. Competence, Asante compatibility, consent and affordable roles must be established during the proposed funded study. All included records are public UI observations and contain no participant-level information.

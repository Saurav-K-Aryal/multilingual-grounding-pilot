This pilot separates question language, supplied English evidence, and cultural image use with 234 recorded requests to two local model configurations. AfriQA contributes 24 locked Yoruba and Swahili questions, while Afri-MCQA contributes 12 Yoruba image groups from public labeled development records. Strict native-question English-span accuracy is 3/12 for Yoruba and 7/12 for Swahili with Qwen3.5, and 7/12 and 6/12 with Gemma4; differences from the published English questions are mixed. Both models return null for all 24 empty-passage controls. With native cultural questions and options, accuracy rises from 2/12 without images to 5/12 with images for Qwen, and from 3/12 to 7/12 for Gemma. These descriptive results support an auditable external-label evaluation interface. Native-language service quality, new community judgments, locality and freshness handling, and efficient evaluation policies remain future work.
